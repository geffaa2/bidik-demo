Bidik: a membership site you can try

A fictional photography school on WordPress and Paid Memberships Pro: plans and a free trial, drip courses, protected content, prorated upgrades, failed-payment recovery, pause and cancel, an owner dashboard and a CSV importer. Everything in it is made up.

Open the live demo

The first load builds the whole school in your browser (WordPress runs in WebAssembly), so give it a minute. Nothing is sent anywhere, and closing the tab throws it away.

  • You start signed in as Rani, a Member. The bar at the top lets you view as a Free member, a Pro, a member whose card failed, or the owner.
  • The Demo clock moves time forward 1, 3 or 7 days and runs what would have run: renewals, reminders, modules opening.
  • Test cards: 4242 4242 4242 4242 works; 4000 0000 0000 0341 works now and fails on renewal.
  • It runs on a built-in test gateway that behaves like Stripe's test mode. On a real project your own Stripe account is connected.
